5. Apple Health (HealthKit), Apple Watch and other watches

On iPhone the app connects to Apple Health only if you switch it on and allow it in iOS. It may read: heart rate (during a workout and as saved by your watch), body weight, body fat and lean body mass, sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, workouts saved by other apps, workout calories and your date of birth (for heart-rate zones). It may write: finished workouts (with heart rate and an estimate of calories) and weigh-ins.

Data from Apple Health is used only for the app’s features (showing it next to your training, completing your log). It is not used for advertising or marketing, not sold and not shared with third parties (other than the hosting provider that stores your account, if it becomes part of your training log), and we do not store it in iCloud. You can revoke access on your iPhone: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Habcore Gym.

Apple Watch talks directly to your iPhone (it shows the running workout and sends ticked sets and heart rate). Garmin and Wear OS watches – where available in your version – connect to your phone. All of this happens only if you connect a watch.

6. Trainer mode (gym) – what the consent means

When you join a gym with its gym code, or take over an account a trainer made for you, you consent as follows: “By joining the gym you give every trainer of this gym (now and in future) full access to your training account: they can see it and lead it, which means adding, changing and deleting workouts, exercises, sets, plans, measurements and body weight. Every change a trainer makes is logged with their name. A trainer cannot change your password or e-mail and cannot delete your account. This consent cannot be withdrawn on its own: you can only leave the gym, which ends the trainers’ access at once.”

To be clear: “cannot be withdrawn on its own” only means the app has no separate switch for “withdraw the consent but stay in the gym”. You can withdraw the consent at any time – by leaving the gym (Settings → Account → Gym → Leave the gym) or by writing to us; the trainers lose access at once. Withdrawal does not affect what was done before. You always keep the right to delete your account (section 10). An account a trainer made (without a password) is led by the trainer until you take it over with the invite link – at which point you accept the Terms, this policy and the consents.

During live and group training the client’s running workout is kept on the server so that the trainer and the client see it at the same time; when it ends it goes into the client’s log.

7. Recipients and processors

We do not sell data and do not pass it to anyone for their own purposes. The app does not use any artificial intelligence (AI) services and passes no data to AI providers. There are no integrations with other services either (e.g. Strava, webhooks, backups to external storage) and no access to data through API tokens. Data may reach only:

  • The hosting provider – the server the app runs on (it stores the accounts and training data). See the box below.
  • The trainers of your gym – only if you join it (section 6).
  • Push notification services – if you turn on notifications in a browser: the (encrypted) notification goes through your browser’s push service (e.g. Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, Mozilla for Firefox). The iPhone app uses local notifications, without our server.
  • Map services – only if you search for a gym on the map in the gym reminder: the text you type and a rough area go from your phone to Apple (iPhone) or to OpenStreetMap/Photon (komoot), and map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. Your location never reaches our server.
  • Hevy – only if you import your history from Hevy yourself: your device connects directly to Hevy with your Hevy API key, downloads the history and forgets the key; we send nothing to Hevy.
  • Your calendar app (e.g. Google, Apple, Outlook) – only if you add the calendar link to it: the calendar fetches your plan (and, if you choose, finished workouts) from our server.
  • YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) – only if you open a YouTube video attached to an exercise.
  • Apple – if you use Apple Health, the data in Apple Health is kept by Apple under its own terms.
  • Public authorities – only where the law requires it.

Accounts and training data are stored in the European Union. This relies on the European Commission’s adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework – for companies certified under it) or on standard contractual clauses; write to us for a copy of the safeguards.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and training data – until you delete the account. Deleting the account removes from the server at once: the account, the training log, photos and videos, gym membership, the change log and any live-workout state.
  • Server backups – up to 30 days, then overwritten.
  • Security event log – up to 90 days (no IP addresses).
  • Web server logs (with IP addresses) – up to 14 days.
  • The invite code and the date of your acceptance – while the account exists; where needed to defend against claims – until they are time-barred.
  • Correspondence with us – up to 1 year after the matter is closed (complaints – until the limitation periods expire).

10. Your rights

You have the right of access to your data and a copy, rectification, erasure (“right to be forgotten”), restriction of processing, data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing).

  • Deleting your account – yourself: Settings → Account → Delete my account (in the browser and in the mobile app), or by e-mail to us.
  • Export (portability) – yourself: Settings → Data & backup (JSON backup, CSV files).
  • Rectification – you can correct most data yourself in the app.
  • Withdrawing the health-data consent – the training log cannot work without it, so withdrawing it means deleting the account (in the app or by writing to us). You withdraw the trainers’ consent by leaving the gym, and push consent in your browser or phone settings.

We answer requests without undue delay, at the latest within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland, uodo.gov.pl) or with the supervisory authority where you live.

11. Profiling, ads, analytics

We make no automated decisions with legal effects and do not profile you. There are no ads, trackers, analytics or tracking pixels. Suggestions in the app (e.g. the next weight) are calculated by the app from your own workouts, for you only.

12. Cookies and device storage

We use only strictly necessary mechanisms: the sign-in session cookie (and a short-lived cookie while a trainer leads a client’s account), browser storage (localStorage, IndexedDB) and the service worker cache, where the app keeps your settings, data for offline use and its own files. We use no analytics or advertising cookies, so we do not ask for consent to them. Fonts, pictures and exercise animations load from our server, not from third-party services.

13. Children

The app is meant for people aged 16 or older. A younger person may use it only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who accepts the Terms, this policy and the health-data consent on their behalf. If we learn that a child created an account without such consent, we will delete it.

14. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored as scrypt hashes, sign-in attempts are rate-limited, sessions can be ended on all devices, and only authorised people have access to the server.

15. Changes

We announce changes in the app at least 14 days before they take effect (a change of hosting provider in section 7 – without delay). If a change concerns consents, we ask for them again at sign-in. Earlier versions are available on request.
